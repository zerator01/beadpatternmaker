Perler bead designs

Thirty free perler bead designs you can open in the maker, resize, recolour and print. The designs are original charts built for 5 mm midi beads, from a 10-minute keychain to a full 29 × 29 pegboard piece. Pick a shape, open it in the maker, then switch the palette if you want the same design in Hama or Artkal colours.

Pick a size

Match the design to the board you own

Most of the designs here are 11–15 beads across. A 29 × 29 board gives you room to scale a small design up; a 10 × 10 board is best for a single motif.

BoardBest forApprox. finished sizeTime
10 × 10Keychain, magnet, hair clip5 × 5 cm10–20 min
16 × 16Small character, pin8 × 8 cm20–40 min
29 × 29Standard square board14.5 × 14.5 cm1–2 hours
48 × 48Large wall piece24 × 24 cm3–5 hours
64 × 64Statement piece32 × 32 cm6+ hours

Finished sizes assume 5 mm midi beads and no gaps between beads. Mini beads are about 2.6 mm; maxi beads are about 10 mm.

Make it yours

Three ways to use a design

1

Open it in the maker

Click “Use this design” and the chart opens in the editor. Paint individual beads, erase the background or remap the whole thing to another palette.

2

Resize it for your board

A design is a starting point, not a fixed file. Change the grid width, then fix the few cells that need it around the eyes or outline.

3

Print the coded chart

Download the PDF for a numbered chart with a bead list. Print at 100% if you want the grid to match the physical board.
