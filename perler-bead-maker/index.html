The editor

What makes this perler bead maker different

Automatic conversion is the easy part. The work is in the twenty minutes after it: fixing an eye, dropping a colour you ran out of, and printing a chart you can follow.

1

A code in every cell

You should never have to guess whether a square is peach or light pink. Every cell carries the palette code, and the legend gives the name and count.

2

Edit before you place

Paint, erase or pick a bead straight on the chart. Undo covers every stroke, so a mis-click costs nothing.

3

Print a chart, not a picture

The PDF gives you a grid, a heavier line every five beads, tiled pages for large patterns and a final bead list.

Questions

Perler bead maker questions

Is this perler bead maker free?
Yes. The maker and the exports are free, with no account, no watermark and no upload. Your photo is processed in the browser.
Can I use a phone photo?
Yes. A well-lit photo with one clear subject works best. Crop tight to the subject and remove a plain background if the photo has one.
Can I edit the chart after it is made?
Yes. Click a bead to paint it, erase it or pick up its colour. The bead counts update live, so you can see exactly how a change affects the shopping list.
Which palette should I choose?
Choose the brand you actually own. Perler, Hama and Artkal have different colour ranges; switching the palette remaps the chart to the closest beads in that set.
