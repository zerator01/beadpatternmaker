Which fuse bead palette should you use?

The right palette is the one you can actually buy. Switch it before you export and the chart remaps to the closest available colour in that set.

1

Perler-style

The US workhorse. A broad, familiar range with plenty of brights, pastels and neutrals for character and animal designs.

2

Hama-style

Common in Europe. Similar bead size, slightly different colour names and availability. Choose it if your stash is Hama.

3

Artkal-style

A large colour range that is popular for detailed pieces. The chart will use the closest Artkal-style beads rather than the Perler set.

Questions

Fuse bead pattern maker questions

Are fuse beads and perler beads the same?
They are the same type of craft bead: small plastic barrels placed on a pegboard and fused with heat. Perler, Hama and Artkal are different brands with different colour ranges, but the grid and bead size are usually compatible.
Can I use this maker for Hama beads?
Yes. Choose the Hama-style palette before you export and the chart will use the closest Hama colours. Check a real bead against the screen swatch before a large project.
What grid size should I use?
29 × 29 matches a standard square pegboard. Use 16 × 16 for a keychain or magnet and 48 × 48 or 64 × 64 for a larger wall piece.
Can I sell what I make?
The patterns you generate from your own photos are yours to use. The site's original gallery designs are free to make for personal use; if you plan to sell finished pieces, use your own source images and check the terms of the bead brand you use.
