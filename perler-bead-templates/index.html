Perler bead templates

Free printable perler bead templates for the days when you want to draw your own design instead of converting a photo. Each template is a blank grid with a heavier line every five beads, sized for a standard pegboard and set up to print at 100% on A4. Use the 10 × 10 or 16 × 16 grid for a keychain, the 29 × 29 for a standard square board, and the 48 × 48 or 64 × 64 when you are planning a larger piece. Draw one cell at a time, or colour the squares in before you place the beads.

Size guide

Which template size should I print?

Choose by finished size first, then by how much detail you need. A larger grid gives more detail but takes longer to fill.

Finished sizes assume 5 mm midi beads and no gaps. If you use mini or maxi beads, the finished size changes even though the grid does not.

Printing

How to print a template so the grid matches your board

1

Print at 100%

Turn off “fit to page” in the print dialog. The PDF is set up for A4; on Letter paper, choose “actual size” and check the first page with a ruler.

2

Check one cell

Measure a single square. For a 5 mm bead board, one cell should be 5 mm across. If it is not, the print scale is wrong.

3

Tape the sheets

For 48 × 48 and 64 × 64, overlap the printed edges and tape along the grid lines. The page footer tells you which columns and rows you are holding.
